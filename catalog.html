Where to Find Every Secret

a secret-seeker's guide — ✨ spoilers! ✨ all 46 secrets and secret places, and how to find them

✧ ❀ ✧

🌳 The Great Forest (round your cottage)

  • 🌳 Coin-Filled Knothole — an old stump about 40 steps north-east of your cottage. Stand right next to it and hold F (or press E)
  • 🪨 Under the Loose Stone — about 35 steps south-west of the school. A wobbly stone; hold F right beside it
  • 💧 Glint in the Puddle — about 35 steps west of your cottage. Hold F at the puddle
  • 📚 The Book That Isn't a Book — inside the school library: E at the bookcase to pull the red book
  • 🔔 The Bell's Secret — ring the school bell (E) THREE times, quickly. A trapdoor opens nearby — E to climb down
  • 🎶 The Song That Opens the Oak — the giant oak about 45 steps south-east of your cottage. Stand near it and play 2, 4, 2, 1 on your flute (U). Then step through the root door
  • 🪞 The Still Pond's Island — the pond about 40 steps west of your cottage. Stand at its east edge and hold F. Stepping stones appear — hop out to the chest
  • 🌙 The Door That Comes Out at Night — a crooked tree about 40 steps south-west of your cottage. Only at NIGHT a little door appears: E to knock
  • 🌫️ The Village in the Fog — on early MORNINGS a fog patch sits about 40 steps north-east of the school. Walk into the fog
  • 🗺️ X Marks the Spot — collect all 6 map scraps (2 in Reedmere, 2 in Golden Hollow, 1 in Frostpine, 1 in Silverveil — each lies a short walk from where you arrive). Then a big X appears about 70 steps north-east of your cottage: E to dig

🏙️ The Cities

  • 🐈 Follow the Shop Cat (Golden Hollow) — pop into the General Store and back out. Biscuit slinks round the side; FOLLOW her (stay close, or she gives up!). She climbs a ladder at the back — E to climb up to the attic, and open the chest
  • 💌 Messages in Bottles (Reedmere) — a bottle washes up on the south-east shore, a new one every day. Read all five; bottle 4 tells you where Captain Tilly's chest is: a hidden cove across the lagoon to the north-west
  • 🗝️ The Back Rooms (Reedmere, Golden Hollow, Frostpine) — chat (💬) with a market shopkeeper 3 times on different visits. A 🗝️ back-room button appears in their shop
  • 👂 The Gossips — a purple-hooded Gossip stands in every city square. Listen to her and she gives you a hint for a secret you haven't found

❄️ Frostpine (12 secrets!)

  • 🐾 Mysterious Tracks — little tracks lead from the square out to the WEST edge of town. Follow them to the snowy den and E to peek in
  • 🧊 Behind the Frozen Waterfall — the frozen waterfall tumbles down the hill on the east-north-east edge of town. Hold F at the crack at its foot, then E to squeeze through
  • ⛄ The Snowman's Secret — the lonely snowman south-east of the square. Pat him (E) a few times
  • 🐟 Fish Frozen in Time — a big block of ice to the south. Hold F beside it
  • ♨️ The Hidden Hot Spring — follow the steam deep into the pines to the west-south-west. E to dip your paws (full hearts!)
  • 🛷 Grandpa Frost's Old Sled — a snowdrift to the north with a red runner poking out. Hold F by the runner
  • 🪨 The Rune Stone — a carved stone to the east-north-east. Hold F to read it — it tells you the icicle tune
  • 🎐 The Icicle Chimes — three icicles beside the rune stone. Tap (E) the LONG one, then the SHORT one, then the MIDDLE one
  • 🦉 The Watching Owls — three carved owls. Study each one (E); then go where they are ALL looking (south-south-east) and dig (E)
  • 🧤 The Snow Giant's Mitten — something huge and red in a drift to the north-west. Hold F right beside it
  • 🌲 The Hollow Pine — an old pine to the east with a knothole. Hold F at the knothole
  • ⛸️ The Lost Skate Blade — a silver glint in the snow north-east of where you arrive, on the way to the Frozen Lake. Hold F on it

🗝️ The 19 Secret Places

  • 🖼️ Into the Painting — an easel in a sunny clearing about 50 steps south of your cottage. Hold F at the painting, then E to step in
  • 🪣 The Whispering Well — an old well about 45 steps east of the school. Hold F to listen, then E to climb down
  • ☁️ Treehouse Above the Clouds — the tallest tree, far east of your cottage. Hold F by the trunk to spot the rope ladder, then E
  • 🦉 The Owls' Night Library — a tree far south-west of your cottage that glows only at NIGHT. E at the glowing hollow
  • 🌙 The Moon Garden — a ring of white stones far south of your cottage. Walk into the middle at NIGHT
  • 🧚 The Fairy Market — a mushroom ring far west of your cottage. Walk into it at DUSK, when the mushrooms glow
  • ✨ Glow-Worm Tunnels — a rooty burrow far north-east of your cottage. Hold F at the hole, then E to crawl in
  • 🧩 The Puzzle Doors — three stone doors north-west of the school. Read the clue stone (E), then push the doors: ☀️ sun, ⭐ star, 🌙 moon
  • 🗝️ The Locked Door in Your Cottage — a tiny door in the back wall of your own cottage. It opens once you have the 🕯️ Seeker's Candle
  • 🏰 The Castle's Secret Passage — in your Great Hall, a crooked torch on the right-hand wall. Hold F at it, then E to pull it

💡 Seeker's Tips

  • Sparkles only show when you're REALLY close — walk slowly near odd things
  • When you're right on top of something, the prompt says “look closely 🔍”. Hold F and a glowing blue frame shows what you found
  • Listen to 3 Gossip rumours and you're given the 🕯️ Seeker's Candle — it flickers faster the closer you are to a secret
  • Your Collection Book (N) has a 🔍 Secrets page that counts what you've found

Dangers and Perils

things that can make you lose hearts — for brave mice only

✧ ❀ ✧

🦅 The Hawk's Shadow💭 a dream

  • A great shadow sweeps over the meadow — DIVE under a leaf, a log or a mushroom before the hawk swoops!
  • Caught in the open: talons graze you (−2 ❤️) and you drop something from your pack

🐍 The Grass Snake💭 a dream

  • A long green snake slithers through the tall grass. Hear the hiss, see the grass ripple
  • Too close and it lunges (−3 ❤️) — but toss it an egg and it'll let you pass

🌵 Thorn Bramble Patches💭 a dream

  • Wicked thorn thickets with a shortcut right through them
  • Push through and they scratch (−1 ❤️ each step) — or find the hidden rabbit run round the side

🥶 Freezing Cold💭 a dream

  • In Frostpine blizzards, your warmth runs out fast
  • Run out of warmth and you lose hearts until you reach a fire, a cocoa den or the hot spring

🌊 Rapids and Whirlpools💭 a dream

  • Fast river rapids sweep you downstream and bump you on rocks (−1 ❤️ per bump)
  • Whirlpools pull you under — swim hard sideways to escape!

🪨 Falling Rocks💭 a dream

  • On the mountain paths, pebbles trickle down first… then BOULDERS
  • Watch for the warning dust, dodge left or right — get hit and −3 ❤️

🐝 Angry Bees💭 a dream

  • Knock a beehive while gathering honey and the whole swarm comes after you
  • Jump into water to escape them, or get stung (−1 ❤️ each sting)

🍄 Poison Mushrooms💭 a dream

  • Some mushrooms LOOK just like the good ones, with tiny tell-tale spots
  • Eat one and feel dizzy — your controls go wobbly and you lose 2 ❤️

🌩️ Lightning Storms💭 a dream

  • On storm days, lightning strikes the tallest thing around
  • Don't stand on hilltops or under lone trees! A near-miss knocks you flat (−4 ❤️)

🕳️ Long Falls💭 a dream

  • Fall from somewhere really high and you land with a THUMP (lose hearts for the height)
  • Glide with a leaf-umbrella or land in water or a haystack to be safe

🐀 The Rat Bandits💭 a dream

  • A gang of rat bandits ambushes travellers on the old roads at night
  • Fight them off with your stick (or run!) — every hit costs a heart, and they want your coins

🔥 Hot Embers💭 a dream

  • Around the forge, the cooking fires and the dragon's nest, sparks and embers fly
  • Step on a hot ember and OUCH (−1 ❤️) — wear the fire-proof boots to walk right over them

👻 The Shadow-Wisps💭 a dream

  • Black wisps with glowing purple eyes drift through the dark places
  • They drain your hearts if they touch you — keep a lantern lit, they hate the light

🕷️ Sticky Spider Webs💭 a dream

  • Huge webs strung between the trees of the Darkwood
  • Get stuck and the spider comes to look (−2 ❤️) — wiggle free fast (tap Space)!

😵 Hungry and Tired💭 a dream

  • Forget to eat for a whole day and your hearts slowly go down
  • Sleep in your own bed to wake up with full hearts again

💚 How to Get Hearts Back💭 a dream

  • Eat cooked food, sit by a fire, soak in the hot spring, sleep in your bed
  • Find golden heart-berries — and a rare Heart Locket that gives you an extra heart forever
