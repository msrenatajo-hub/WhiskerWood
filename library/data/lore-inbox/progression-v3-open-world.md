# WhiskerWood — Progression v3: Knowledge and Abilities, Not Levels
_Captured 2026-10-16 from the user's design message. Refines world-v2-cities-and-progression.md (same 12-phase arc, more detail)._

**Model:** Breath of the Wild / Outer Wilds / Subnautica — the player's KNOWLEDGE and ABILITIES unlock the world, never arbitrary level gates. No XP, no levels.

1. **A Quiet Beginning — Fallen Log School.** Classmates, teachers, the headmaster, the little library, the garden, the workshop. Teach walking, running, climbing, gathering, simple crafting, reading maps — naturally. The player falls in love with the school. Then the expedition begins.
2. **Alone.** A predator attacks; you wake alone; the expedition is gone. Objective: survive. Starting kit (maybe): small backpack, journal, simple knife, lantern, wooden compass from graduation. Learn gathering, food, water, shelter, campfires, basic crafting, avoiding predators. Peaceful with moments of danger.
3. **The Search Begins.** Clues: torn maps, campfires, teacher journals, footprints, broken wagons, dropped equipment — "They were here." **Build Your Home** matters now: expand, decorate, workshops, herbs, storage, cooking, furniture — your safe place.
4. **Exploring the Great Forest.** Aspen Forest, Pine Forest, Wildflower Patch, Meadow, Creek, hidden caves, ancient ruins, Moonkeeper markers. Ability unlocks: Better Rope (higher cliffs), Stronger Lantern (darker caves), Better Backpack (carry more), Climbing Claws (scale bark), Raft (cross rivers), Grappling Vine (cross gaps). Every upgrade expands exploration.
5. **Moonstone Hollow.** Huge, ancient, silent; nobody entered for centuries. You descend.
6. **Roothaven.** Big reveal — the teachers are alive. Engineering, machines, water systems, advanced crafting, new quests. You learn: Everbough is real.
7. **Reconnecting the World.** Mission shifts from survival to reconnecting civilizations — the first traveler between cities in generations. Restore trade, knowledge, mail, academies, friendships, maps, old roads, Moonkeeper stations.
8. **Everbough.** The legendary, largest city: Grand Library, Academy, guilds, politics, huge quests, festivals, dozens of NPCs.
9. **The Other Great Cities** — each opens a new gameplay system: Roothaven (engineering, machinery, underground exploration), Marsh City (boats, navigation, fishing), Plains City (farming, animal care, cooking), Northern City (cold survival, mountaineering, advanced woodworking).
10. **Becoming a Moonkeeper.** Earned, not inherited — by reconnecting the cities, sharing knowledge, restoring trust. Unlocks responsibilities and opportunities no ordinary traveler has.
11. **Restoring the Great Canopy Alliance.** Reopen ancient bridges, underground tunnels, messenger routes, shared libraries, trade caravans, student exchanges between Royal Academies. The world visibly changes as cities reconnect.
12. **Life After the Story.** Dream home, apprentices or family (TBD), collect every book for the Grand Library, museum collections, homes in every city, master every craft, help run festivals, keep discovering.

**Reputation, per Great City** (instead of levels): helping a city builds trust → new buildings, advanced crafting techniques, private library archives, festival invitations, homes opening their doors, new trade goods, guild memberships.

> "By the end of the game, you're not the most powerful mouse in the world — you've become the mouse who reunited it."
