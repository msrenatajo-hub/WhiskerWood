# WhiskerWood — World v2: The Great Cities, No Human House, and the 12-Phase Story
_Design conversation captured 2026-07-01. This SUPERSEDES parts of the current codex (notably the Human House and the family-first story arc). To be folded into data/codex.json via scripts/build-codex.mjs._

## Cities as living places
Cities are not shops-and-menus. Arriving at one should feel like "I could spend hours here."

### 🌳 EVERBOUGH — The City of Knowledge
Warm, lively, full of discovery. Grew UPWARD into the branches of ancient trees.
- **The Grand Library** (its iconic building): books that expand lore, maps to hidden places, recipes from manuscripts, clues to other cities, explorers' journals.
- **Royal Academy** — voluntary classes because they're FUN: Botany (field trips with a professor, herb identification), Cartography (make maps, reveal trails, better compass), Architecture (building pieces, decorations, bridges, furniture), Craftsmanship (advanced recipes).
- **Neighborhoods** — craftsmen, artists, scholars, gardeners, explorers districts.
- **Town Square** — changes daily: markets, street musicians, festivals, children playing, travelers' stories.
- **Seasonal festivals** you PARTICIPATE in: Spring Blossom Festival, Lantern Night, Harvest Feast, Winter Lights — games, contests, decorating, special foods, unique rewards.
- **Visitable houses** — NPCs invite you in; every house tells a story (books, pictures, crafts, heirlooms).

### 🪨 ROOTHAVEN — The City of Builders (full lore)
> "The earth remembers what the sky forgets." — Ancient Roothaven Proverb

One of the Five Great Cities of the **Great Canopy Alliance**. Grew DOWNWARD into the roots of the oldest forests. One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities. Surface mice think it's a myth; Roothaven thinks the surface cities vanished generations ago. Both are wrong.

- **Location**: beneath the Pine Forest. Only known entrance through Moonstone Hollow → ancient Moonkeeper tunnels → Root Caverns → The Great Descent.
- **First impression**: expecting a cave, the tunnel widens — warm golden lights, warmer air, water sounds, distant voices — then an underground metropolis around the roots of an impossibly ancient pine (**the World Pine**), underground rivers, stone bridges over glowing waterways, hundreds of lanterns, ceiling lost in darkness. *This isn't a ruin. People still live here.*
- **The Great Roots**: sacred; homes carved into roots/stone/crystal chambers; roots weave through the city like streets; no one may damage them.
- **Architecture**: solid, ancient, warm, engineered — stone, slate, clay brick, pine roots, polished crystals, bronze, rare iron; rounded arches, massive pillars, water channels everywhere.
- **Light**: no sun — lanterns, crystal lamps, bioluminescent fungi, reflective stone; cozy amber glow.
- **Sound**: flowing water, water wheels, echoing footsteps, hammers, steam, wind through tunnels, children laughing.
- **Districts**: Root Hall (council; oldest root through the ceiling), Foundry District (engineers, water-powered machines, pulleys, elevators), Crystal Quarter (crystal artisans, glassmakers), River District (canals, boats, fishing, cargo, markets), Archive Hall (stone libraries, maps, engineering records, Moonkeeper journals), Hearth District (families, schools, glowing-moss gardens).
- **Government**: Council of Roots — one Elder per district; eldest is the **Root Speaker** (guides discussion, doesn't rule).
- **Culture**: engineering, craftsmanship, reliability, community, history, quiet confidence. "Good work speaks for itself."
- **Economy**: stonework, tools, machinery, mining equipment, water pumps, crystal lamps, strong rope, metal hardware.
- **Transport**: waterways, stone bridges, elevators, spiral stairs, root walkways, cargo lifts.
- **Food**: underground mushrooms, root vegetables, fresh fish, stored berries, nut breads, herb soups, (maybe) crystal cave honey.
- **Academy**: Royal Academy of Roothaven — engineering, architecture, geology, hydrology, mechanics, surveying. Everbough students studied here centuries ago.
- **Crest**: a massive pine root wrapped around a crystal — "Strength below. Light within."
- **Motto**: "Beneath every great forest lies an even older story."
- **The Root Cathedral**: the city's oldest public hall — colossal World Pine roots descend through a castle-sized cavern, twisting into natural arches/bridges/columns, built around for centuries without cutting one; lanterns hang from roots like stars; streams under wooden walkways. The moment the player realizes this is one of the greatest civilizations in the world.
- **The Teachers**: the Fallen Log School expedition accidentally discovered Roothaven via Moonstone Hollow, were welcomed (first surface mice in generations), stayed to learn — never meant to disappear. When the player arrives: joyous reunion (they thought the player died) — then the reveal: "Everbough is real," learned from Roothaven's oldest maps.

### 🌊 Marsh City (Reedmere) — floating homes, boats everywhere; fishing, boat races, navigation challenges, raft building, flooded ruins. Iconic: **The Lighthouse of Reeds**.
### 🌾 Plains City (Golden Hollow) — food capital; cooking competitions, gardens, farms, harvest festivals, animal care. Iconic: **The Great Harvest Hall**.
### ❄️ Northern City (Frostpine) — snow, ice caves, winter survival, hot springs, wood carving, ice sculpting. Iconic: **The Hall of Everfrost** (carved of ice and evergreen wood, preserves the city's history).
### Everbough's iconic building: **The Grand Library**. Roothaven's: **The Great Engine Hall** (powered by underground rivers).

## Across all cities
- **Relationships**: friendships, helping families, birthdays, letters, returning years later, watching children grow.
- **Own a small home** in every city eventually — decorate, store, sleep.
- **Guilds**: Explorer's, Builders, Botanists, Historians, Engineers, Naturalists — unique quests and unlocks each.
- **Museum**: donate plants, fish, artifacts, insects, Moonkeeper relics, maps — fills through your adventures.
- **Art Gallery**: NPC artists paint landscapes/animals/cities/your accomplishments; after a major quest an artist paints YOU and it hangs in the gallery.
- **Cafés & bakeries**: sit, eat, overhear rumors — some quests begin from overheard conversations.

## ❌ The Human House is REMOVED from canon
The world outgrew it. Humans still exist but are experienced from the edges of one **enormous protected wilderness** (national-forest scale — ancient, self-contained mouse civilizations):
- a hiking trail crossing the forest; a summer campsite; a picnic area to scavenge after people leave; a ranger station (too dangerous while occupied); logging roads that changed parts of the forest; fences/bridges/culverts/old farm equipment as giant landmarks.
- Human objects become **discoverable treasures, not destinations**: a lost button = a shield; a marble = a prized decoration; a thimble = a bucket; a spool = a table.

## The 12-phase progression (BotW/Outer Wilds/Subnautica style — knowledge & abilities unlock the world, not levels)
1. **A Quiet Beginning** — life at Fallen Log School: classmates, teachers, headmaster, little library, garden, workshop. Learn naturally (walk/run/climb/gather/craft/read maps). Fall in love with the school. Then: the expedition begins.
2. **Alone** — the predator attacks; you wake alone with a small backpack, journal, simple knife, lantern, and a wooden compass from graduation. Survive: gathering, food, water, shelter, campfires, basic crafting, predator avoidance. Peaceful with moments of danger.
3. **The Search Begins** — torn maps, campfires, teacher journals, footprints, broken wagons, dropped equipment. "They were here."
   - **Build Your Home** — expand, decorate, workshops, herbs, supplies, cooking, furniture.
4. **Exploring the Great Forest** — Aspen/Pine/Wildflower/Meadow/Creek, caves, ruins, Moonkeeper markers. Progression = ability unlocks: Better Rope (cliffs), Stronger Lantern (dark caves), Better Backpack, Climbing Claws (bark), Raft (rivers), Grappling Vine (gaps).
5. **Moonstone Hollow** — huge, ancient, silent; you descend.
6. **Roothaven** — the reveal; teachers alive; engineering/machines/water systems/advanced crafting; "Everbough is real."
7. **Reconnecting the World** — mission shift: restore trade, knowledge, mail, academies, friendships, maps, old roads, Moonkeeper stations. First traveler between cities in generations.
8. **Everbough** — the legendary city: Grand Library, Academy, guilds, politics, huge quests, festivals, dozens of NPCs.
9. **The Other Great Cities** — each introduces a major system: Roothaven engineering/underground; Marsh boats/fishing; Plains farming/cooking; Northern cold survival/mountaineering/woodworking.
10. **Becoming a Moonkeeper** — EARNED, not inherited: by reconnecting cities and restoring trust you're recognized as the first Moonkeeper in generations.
11. **Restoring the Great Canopy Alliance** — reopen bridges, tunnels, messenger routes, shared libraries, caravans, student exchanges. The world visibly changes.
12. **Your Life After the Story** — build the dream home, apprentices/family, complete the Grand Library's books, museum collections, city homes, master crafts, festivals, keep discovering.

## Progression currency: per-city REPUTATION (no XP/levels)
Helping a city builds trust → new buildings, advanced techniques, private archives, festival invitations, homes opening their doors, trade goods, guild memberships. You end not as the most powerful mouse — as **the mouse who reunited the world**. Theme: rebuilding connections across a world that forgot it was ever one civilization.
